Play on Two Phones

No sign-up, no server. One player creates a game code, the other joins with it - send the codes in WhatsApp. Moves then go directly phone to phone.

Works best on the same Wi-Fi. On different networks, phones may need the free Google STUN helper below, and some mobile networks can still block direct play.
